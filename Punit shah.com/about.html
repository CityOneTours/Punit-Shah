My Journey

From a Single Idea to a Legacy of Impact

My entrepreneurial journey has been a continuous process of learning and evolution, driven by a deep-seated belief in service and authenticity. It all started in the UAE with City One Tourism and Travel, a venture born from a passion for connecting people and cultures through carefully crafted travel experiences. That initial venture set the standard for everything that came after, building a foundation on which I could expand my vision.

The path eventually led me from the dynamic landscape of the UAE to the rich potential of Uganda. This transition was more than a geographical move; it was an expansion of my purpose. I saw an opportunity to build businesses that not only succeed but also create meaningful, sustainable impact. Each new venture became a chapter in a larger story of bridging continents, empowering communities, and turning ambitious ideas into reality.

City One Tourism and Travel

We started by creating exceptional travel experiences that connect cultures. Our focus has always been on providing authentic service and building lasting memories for travelers from around the world.

K Hotels

A landmark of Ugandan hospitality, K Hotels rose from its initial construction to become recognized by TripAdvisor as one of the top hotels in Africa. It embodies our commitment to blending luxury with local warmth and excellence.

Yuti Breweriies

As a pioneer in Uganda’s mass-market beer and liquor industry, we built a brand that resonates nationwide. Inaugurated by H.E. the President, Yuti focuses on quality, affordability, and local employment.

Emiboozi

Meaning “conversations” in Luganda, Emiboozi is a restaurant concept that is redefining the Ugandan dining scene. It’s a space where culture, community, and cuisine come together to create a unique lifestyle brand.

Lumilife

Specializing in innovative lighting and automation solutions, Lumilife brings quality and convenience to spaces ranging from homes to large commercial properties, setting new standards in the industry.

Rudra Agro

Focused on the future of agriculture, Rudra Agro trades in cocoa and coffee while strategically acquiring land for cultivation. Our work is driven by fair trade practices and a commitment to impact-driven growth.

ByKurators

ByKurators is a premium lifestyle and luxury retail brand that curates fashion, beauty, and home essentials. Committed to quality, elegance, and innovation, it offers thoughtfully selected collections that inspire modern living and exceptional shopping experiences.

Philosophy

Building Ecosystems, Not Just Companies

I believe true entrepreneurship extends beyond building individual companies; it's about creating interconnected ecosystems where people, opportunities, and purpose can thrive. My philosophy is grounded in three core principles: perseverance, collaboration, and a relentless commitment to excellence. Success is not just about what I build, but about what I enable others to achieve.

"Success isn't just what you build. It's what you enable others to build through you."

— Punit Shah

My Roots & Vision

A Global Mindset with Local Impact

My story is woven between two vibrant cultures: the boundless ambition of the UAE and the fertile, untapped potential of Uganda. This unique blend has shaped my vision to create businesses that operate with a global mindset while delivering a powerful local impact. My goal is to continue building bridges between these two worlds, creating enterprises that transcend borders and foster shared prosperity and growth for years to come.

Rooted in UAE, Building Across Uganda — creating ecosystems of opportunity and growth through perseverance, collaboration, and impact-driven entrepreneurship.

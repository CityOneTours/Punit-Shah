Are travel agencies going to be extinct?

With a plethora of channels to book a travel, are travel agencies going to be extinct? Well the answer is could be…Could be if you are not doing the following!

2 min read
Punit Shah

For centuries, there were only travel agents, then came online sites, the world thought travel agency is going to be extinct, we proved them wrong, and as a U turn, of late, online sites are trying to bring the personal touch as well which is the USP of travel agencies!

How to sustain the intensity of the competition? Excellent customer service is an overused word, everyone is striving to delight their customer. So, it is rather a hygiene factor for a business rather than being a differentiator.

In my view three factors, Infrastructure, Availability and Reachability could differentiate you amidst your peer players in the industry.

1

Infrastructure

Cutting edge technology tools that assist in satisfying customers travel needs, enabling the team to function faster. Not only technology infrastructure provide with the cutting edge business infrastructure too to speed up business processes.

2

Availability

If you are not available when the customers are trying to reach out to you, forget about the sale! It is a cliché yet true! Most of the sale works when you pick the phone when they call!

3

Reachability

Do not complicate clients reaching you. Make it simple – they are already tired of waiting in big lines. After their first interaction, if you can give them a personalized number like a mobile number to reach you. At City One we believe in these three, we have the infrastructure of a big company in our mid-size company. Being neither intimidatingly big where one tends to get lost, nor too small is our strategic sweet spot, enduring our sustainability!

What according to you are the differentiating factors? What works for you?

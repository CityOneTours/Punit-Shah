I’M PUNIT SHAH ENTREPRENEUR

Building Sustainable Businesses That Create Meaningful Impact

I’m Punit Shah, a passionate entrepreneur and investor dedicated to building sustainable businesses that create meaningful, lasting impact. My journey began in the UAE and has since expanded across Uganda, where I’ve been at the forefront of ventures in hospitality, beverages, lighting & automation, and agriculture. Each business is built on a foundation of perseverance, collaboration, and the power of purpose-led growth.

Ventures

City One Tourism and Travel

We are dedicated to providing a complete and seamless travel experience. From visa services and hotel bookings to airport transfers, our team ensures every journey is smooth and memorable.

“Creating moments of joy, travel, and togetherness.”

Explore City One Tourism and Travel

K Hotels

in Entebbe, K Hotels merges luxury with local warmth. Recognized by TripAdvisor as one of Africa's top hotels, it stands as a symbol of Ugandan hospitality and excellence.

“A symbol of excellence, comfort, and community.”

Explore K Hotels

Yuti Breweriies

As Uganda’s pioneering mass-market beer and liquor producer, Yuti Breweries focuses on innovation, quality, and affordability while empowering local employment.

“Born in Uganda, brewed for Uganda.”

Explore Yuti Breweriies

Emiboozi

Meaning “conversations” in Luganda, Emiboozi is a restaurant concept celebrating culture, community, and cuisine. We’re building a lifestyle brand that brings people together.

“Where every meal starts a conversation.”

Explore Emiboozi

Lumilife

Specializing in lighting and automation, Lumilife delivers quality, transparency, and innovation. We add value to residential and commercial spaces with smart, convenient solutions.

“Smart lighting, seamless automation, exceptional service.”

Explore Lumilife

Rudra Agro

Focused on the trade of cocoa and coffee, Rudra Agro is driven by transparency, quality, and support for farmers through fair trade practices as we cultivate for the future.

“Trading today, cultivating the future.”

Explore Rudra Agro

White Rose

A Dubai-based trading and distribution company, Whiterose General Trading LLC provides high-quality food items, yarn, and more, building strong partnerships across the Middle East.

“Quality products, trusted partnerships, seamless distribution.”

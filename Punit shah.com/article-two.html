Givers Gain!

UIt was a cold winter morning. The conference room in JW Marriott Marquee hotel was filled with 30+ entrepreneurs. There came an announcement that the next to speak was 'Punit Shah, City One Tourism & Travel'. I cleared my throat, to speak for 30 seconds! Boy, how daunting it was! There were two challenges, to talk, and to talk about your business in 30 seconds! It was for a BNI meeting.

3 min read
Punit Shah

Business Network International acronym’ed as BNI, is your extended army that works for your business. In turn, you work for them too. When you are with BNI, you have the unique opportunity of representing 40+ other business in your network.

Be it your friend setting up an office, be it your B2B partner implementing a software solution, be it your supplier looking for office furniture, you tend to refer your chapter members and advocate for them vociferously, for you have been hearing about their business, their capabilities and their client testimonials every week. You are almost part of their executive committee!

On the early days of City One, most of our first clients were family, friends and friends of friends... those days little did I know that the most successful business relies on referral marketing and relational business transactions rather than on cold calls and the likes.

BNI as an organization has been the pillar in helping me understand and develop my limited understanding on this beautiful concept.

It is nostalgic to recollect the journey, from the days I had cold feet to speak to to the days where I managed to set my foot on the givers gain millionaire club of BNI.

It works well within the Organization too, at City One we believe in giving more to our clients for it is in giving that we receive! Rather than what is in it for us, seeing what is in it for them is the DNA of the organization helping us sustain our delighted customers, leading to client advocacy and ecstasy!

Relationships last longer than transactions…

If a tree is cut...

Uganda, the country I grew up with, kind of a second home. Penned down few facts that excited me...

2 min read
Punit Shah
1

An initiative towards sustainability

Uganda is a tree loving nation and has strict rules regarding cutting the trees. Do you know that Uganda has a unique rule? The rule is, if you cut one tree, you need to plant three additional trees. There's the reason why Uganda isn't too polluted!

2

Commute made easy

In Uganda, motor cycles are used as taxis to ferry people from one place to another. In East Africa and more commonly in Uganda, these motorcycle taxis are called 'boda-boda' or 'boda'. This is one of the cheapest modes of transport within and between cities.

3

A pioneer in media independence

Uganda is one of the few countries that boasts a vibrant media sector. The country nearly has 200 private radio stations and dozens of television stations and print outlets, giving it the ability to boast of its Media sector. Uganda is a pioneer in the liberalization of the media in Africa.

4

Culturally yours...

Uganda is Africa's cultural microcosm. The Ndere Centre is the Home of Cultures and headquarters of Uganda Development Theatre Association is built on 9 acres of well-maintained green, beautifully flowered walk ways and shaded by very mature fruit and other African trees.

It's Troupe captures the amazing cultural songs, dances and unique instruments from the 56 tribes, binding them to make informative stories and a spectacle for the eyes.

5

1008 Species of birds

Uganda boasts a national list of 1008 species of birds. Uganda has more bird species per square kilometer than any other country in Africa? This figure represents more than half the bird species that can be found in the whole of Africa.
